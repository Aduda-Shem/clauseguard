renew for successive twelve (12) month terms without notice unless
terminated in accordance with this Section.

exceed the total fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.

3. INDEMNIFICATION. Each party shall mutually indemnify the other for
third-party claims arising from its own negligence.

4. PAYMENT TERMS. Fees are due net 30 from invoice date.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY. Confidential information remains protected for three
(3) years post-termination.

6. GOVERNING LAW. Governed by the laws of the State of California.

7. DATA PRIVACY. Vendor shall notify Customer of any data breach within
seventy-two (72) hours of discovery.
