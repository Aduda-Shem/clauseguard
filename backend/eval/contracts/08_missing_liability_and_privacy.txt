SIMPLE VENDOR AGREEMENT

1. TERM. This Agreement is effective for one (1) year from the date
signed by both parties.

2. SCOPE OF WORK. Vendor shall deliver the services described in Exhibit
A according to the schedule set out therein.

3. PAYMENT. Client shall pay Vendor net 30 following receipt of each
invoice.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each party agrees to keep the other's confidential
information private for three (3) years after this Agreement ends.

5. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
Ohio.

6. GENERAL. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the
parties and supersedes all prior discussions.
