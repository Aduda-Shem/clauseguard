PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. TERM. Fixed term of one (1) year, no automatic renewal.

2. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Except for fraud, gross negligence, or willful
misconduct, for which liability shall not be limited, each party's
liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid in the
twelve (12) months preceding the claim. This is a standard, narrowly
scoped carve-out and does not represent uncapped liability generally.

3. INDEMNIFICATION. Each party shall mutually indemnify the other for
third-party claims arising from breach of this Agreement.

4. PAYMENT TERMS. Invoices are due net 30.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY. Confidential information shall remain protected for
three (3) years following termination.

6. GOVERNING LAW. Governed by the laws of the State of Virginia.

7. DATA PRIVACY. Provider shall notify Client of any personal data breach
within seventy-two (72) hours of discovery.
