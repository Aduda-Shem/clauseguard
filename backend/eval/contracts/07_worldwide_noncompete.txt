CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

1. TERM. This Agreement runs for one (1) year with no automatic renewal.

2. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Liability shall not exceed the fees paid.

3. INDEMNIFICATION. Each party shall mutually indemnify the other for
third-party claims.

4. PAYMENT TERMS. Invoices are due net 30.

5. NON-COMPETE. Contractor agrees not to engage in any competing business
worldwide for a period of five (5) years following termination of this
Agreement.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY. Confidentiality obligations survive for three (3)
years.

7. GOVERNING LAW. Governed by the laws of the State of Washington.

8. DATA PRIVACY. Contractor shall notify Client of any data breach within
seventy-two (72) hours.
