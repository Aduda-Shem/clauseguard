MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Acme
Vendor Inc. ("Vendor") and Customer Co. ("Customer").

1. TERM. This Agreement has a fixed term of twelve (12) months from the
Effective Date and does not renew automatically. Either party may negotiate
a new term in writing before expiration.

2. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Except for breaches of confidentiality, IP
infringement, or gross negligence, each party's total liability under this
Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid in the twelve (12) months
preceding the claim.

3. INDEMNIFICATION. Each party shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless
the other party from third-party claims arising from its own breach of this
Agreement or grossly negligent acts. This indemnification obligation is
mutual.

4. PAYMENT TERMS. Customer shall pay all invoices within thirty (30) days
of receipt (net 30). Late payments accrue interest at 1% per month.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. Vendor retains ownership of all pre-existing and
background intellectual property. Deliverables created specifically for
Customer under a Statement of Work are assigned to Customer upon full
payment.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each party shall protect the other's confidential
information using reasonable care. Confidentiality obligations survive for
three (3) years following termination of this Agreement.

7. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
Delaware, and the parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of its
courts.

8. DATA PRIVACY. Vendor shall implement reasonable administrative and
technical safeguards for any personal data processed under this Agreement,
and shall notify Customer of any data breach within seventy-two (72) hours
of discovery, consistent with applicable data protection law.

9. ASSIGNMENT. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior
written consent of the other party, not to be unreasonably withheld.
