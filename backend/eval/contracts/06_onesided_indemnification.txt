STAFFING SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. TERM. Fixed term of one (1) year, no automatic renewal.

2. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Liability shall not exceed the fees paid in
the preceding six months.

3. INDEMNIFICATION. Client shall have the sole obligation to indemnify
Vendor against any and all third-party claims, regardless of the cause,
and Vendor shall bear no such obligation toward Client.

4. PAYMENT TERMS. Invoices are due net 30.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY. Confidentiality obligations survive for three (3)
years post-termination.

6. GOVERNING LAW. Governed by the laws of the State of Georgia.

7. DATA PRIVACY. Vendor shall report any personal data breach within
seventy-two (72) hours.
